Privacy Policy

Sunny Isle Tycoon · Last updated: November 25, 2026

The short version

We (the developer of Sunny Isle Tycoon) do not collect, share or sell any personal information: no names, emails, locations or advertising IDs. You can play the whole game offline.

The Android app has three optional extras that use the internet: the Top Players leaderboard (a made-up player name you pick, plus your best score), bonus videos (Google AdMob) and the shop (Google Play). The game also keeps an online backup of your park and sends anonymous play stats (which you can turn off). This page explains what they do.

What the game stores

Your park progress (money, upgrades, your character's outfit, your penthouse furniture, settings and which shop items you own) is saved on your own device, inside the app's private storage.

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this save in your own Google account backup. That backup is controlled by Google and your device settings, not by us.

Online backup of your park

So you don't lose your park if you get a new phone, the game keeps a copy of your save on our server. It uploads the save at most every 2 minutes while you play and when you leave the app. The copy contains only game progress (pretend money, rides, maps, outfits, pets, coins and your daily reward streak), the game version, and, if you joined Top Players, your player name and its secret code so your name comes with you. It is linked only to a long random code made on your device (the server keeps a scrambled copy). There is no account, name or email.

In Settings → Back up progress the game shows an 8-character recovery code. Anyone who has that code can load a copy of the park once, so keep it to yourself. On a new phone, use Settings → Restore from code. Each code works one time.

The backup is stored with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in the United States. We use it only to give you your park back, never for ads, and we never sell it. Backups that are not used for a year may be deleted.

Anonymous play stats

To find out which parts of the game are fun and where players get stuck, the game sends simple anonymous stats: when the game is opened and for how long, tutorial steps, ride levels at a few milestones (1, 10, 25, 50, 100), finished missions, maps bought, using the walk, boat or seaplane, daily rewards, welcome-back earnings and whether they were doubled, which bonus video was watched, which shop item was bought (never any payment details), and whether the game switched to low graphics because it was running slowly. Each event also has the game version, the type of device (Android, iPhone or web) and the time.

The stats are linked only to a random install code made by the game the first time it runs. It is not your advertising ID or any other phone ID, and it is not linked to Top Players or your backup. The stats contain no names, emails, locations or anything you type. They are sent about once a minute and are thrown away if you are offline. We look at them only as totals (for example "how many players finish the tutorial"), never for ads, and we never sell or share them. They are stored with Supabase in the United States and kept for up to 13 months.

You can turn this off any time in Settings → Anonymous play stats. Turning it off stops all stats and deletes the stats this device sent before.

Optional Top Players leaderboard

Top Players is a list of the best parks. It is off until you choose to join it by picking a player name (a nickname of 3 to 16 letters and numbers). Please do not use your real name. Names with rude or mean words are blocked, every name must be different, and we may hide or change any name.

If you join, the game sends our leaderboard server: your player name, your best score (the total pretend money your park has earned), how many maps you own, and a long random code made on your device so the server knows the score is yours. The server keeps only a scrambled copy of that code. Like any internet request, your IP address is used to deliver it; our provider may keep it for a short time in its server logs, and we do not use it for anything. We do not collect your real name, email, location or any other details.

Your player name, score and number of maps are shown to everyone who opens Top Players. The leaderboard is stored with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in the United States. We use this information only to run the leaderboard, never for ads, and we never sell it.

Optional bonus videos (Google AdMob)

In a few places you can choose to watch a short video ad to get a bonus (double income for a while, a bigger helicopter gift, or double welcome-back earnings). Videos only play when you tap a button to watch one. There are no banner ads and no ads that pop up by themselves.

The videos are provided by Google AdMob. We have set every ad request as made for children (child-directed treatment under COPPA, and "under the age of consent" for GDPR), limited to G-rated ads, and asking for non-personalised ads only. This means Google does not use the information below to build a profile of you or show you targeted ads.

To show a video, Google's software on your device may send Google things like your device's IP address, device model and operating system version, the app's name, and diagnostic information (for example, whether a video loaded). It may also read the device's advertising ID; because every request is marked as made for children, it is not used for personalised advertising. Google uses this to deliver the video, count views, and prevent fraud. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in a region where the law requires it, the app may show Google's privacy choices screen, and you can reopen it any time from Settings → Ad privacy.

Optional purchases (Google Play)

The shop sells two optional items: the Park Pass and the Starter Pack. You never have to buy anything to play. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play: we never see your name, email address or payment details. Google tells the app only which items your account owns, so it can unlock them (and restore them if you reinstall). Google Play's own privacy policy applies to the payment. Ask a parent or guardian before buying anything.

What the game does not do

  • No sign-ins, passwords or email addresses. (A Top Players name is just a nickname, not an account.)
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking tools, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no personalised ads. (Our own play stats are anonymous and can be turned off, see above.)
  • No chat or messages between players. If you join Top Players, other players can see only your player name, score and number of maps.
  • No real-money gambling. All coins and cash in the game are pretend and cannot be turned back into real money.

Children

Sunny Isle Tycoon is suitable for everyone, including children. We do not collect personal information from anyone. The backup and play stats contain only game information linked to random codes, not to a person. Top Players uses only a made-up nickname that we filter for rude words, and it asks players not to use their real name. The bonus videos are set up following Google Play's Families policy: child-directed, G-rated and non-personalised, from Google's certified ad network.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset Park in the game, or uninstall the app. Both remove your save from the device. Reset Park also replaces your online backup with the new, empty park within a few minutes.

To stop play stats and delete the ones already sent, turn off Settings → Anonymous play stats. To have your online backup deleted, email us your recovery code from Settings → Back up progress and we will delete it.

To remove yourself from Top Players, open Goals → Top Players → Change → Leave Top Players. This deletes your player name and score from our server straight away. You (or a parent) can also email us your player name and we will delete it.

Changes
