Build your own sunny island park

An idle park game where your island keeps growing even while you're away. Build rides, unlock new islands, then jump in and explore it all yourself.

What you can do

  • 🎢 Build rides from the Sunshine Slide to the mighty Solar Flare coaster.
  • 🏝️ Unlock islands: a beach resort, a trattoria, a lucky coin island and apartment towers.
  • 🚶 Explore as a guest: ride the rides, drive cars, swim and play laser tag.
  • 🏠 Decorate your penthouse with over 20 pieces of furniture.
  • 👕 Style your character with hats, capes, wings and jetpacks.
  • ⭐ Play offline, at your own pace. No pop-up or banner ads.
